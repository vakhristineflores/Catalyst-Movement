Questions people usually ask on a call

Answered here, so you don't have to wait.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. The Affiliate program is free and open to anyone, and so is applying to be an Ambassador.

What's the difference between Affiliate and Ambassador?

Affiliates share a promo code and earn 5% on every successful referral, with no commitments. Ambassadors earn higher rewards and get extras like a business feature, a monthly business audit and marketing materials. In return, they share consistent content and make 2 introductions or 1 successful referral a month. Ambassadors go through a quick brand and audience review first.

What counts as a successful referral?

Someone you refer who buys a Catalyst package using your promo code.

How and when do I get paid?

Once a referral is confirmed as successful, we email you with the details on how to claim your reward.

Do I need a big audience?

Not for the Affiliate program. If you know even one business owner who needs help, you can start. The Ambassador program is for people with an audience or community, which is why there's a brand and audience review.

Do I need to get on a call?

No, for Affiliate and Ambassador you can apply right here. White Label is the only program we set up on a call, because we plan it around your clients.

With White Label, will my clients know about Catalyst?

No. You offer the services under your own brand, we fulfill the work behind the scenes, and your clients stay yours.

What do my referrals actually get?

A free 4-hour trial project, pre-vetted Digital Professionals, a dedicated project manager and access to the whole Catalyst talent pool. Most teams are up and running in 2 days, with plans from $249.
